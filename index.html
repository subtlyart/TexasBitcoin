What is bitscorn?

A meme series that refuses to be dumb all the way down. Each post pairs a Texas tall-tale with a true, sourced explainer of how Bitcoin actually works — mining, energy, grids, governance — so the joke earns the follow and the facts make it stick.

No price calls, no shilling, no "this is financial advice." Just the most Texan parts of Bitcoin, explained the slow way, starring a longhorn who has never once panic-sold.

Educational only. Dale is a cartoon longhorn and manages neither your keys nor a power plant.
